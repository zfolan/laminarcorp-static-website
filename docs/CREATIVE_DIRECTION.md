# Laminar corporate website — creative direction

## Creative brief

Laminar’s corporate site should make one idea tangible: a wealth manager can assess a household as one portfolio and carry a decision all the way to a reviewable account-level implementation plan. The site is for advisors, portfolio managers, practices, firms, and investment teams. Its job is to make the breadth of that connected workflow immediately understandable without turning the product into a technical diagram.

The visual system is quiet, dark, and exact. Large editorial type and generous negative space create the corporate tone; operational mono type appears only when the visitor is looking at portfolio evidence. Status colors are semantic, not decorative. The memorable element is a scroll-controlled coordinated-flow composition in which account rows merge into a household decision and resolve back into precise account actions.

The deliberate design risk is structural rather than ornamental: product data becomes the main cinematic material. There are no generic stock photographs, glass cards, neon tunnels, particles, or disconnected dashboard collages.

Instrument Sans carries editorial and interface language. IBM Plex Mono carries values, allocations, account identifiers, tickers, and operational metadata. The site uses a wide responsive grid, hairline rules, restrained radius, and one subtle radial lift behind the film stage.

## Complete page-copy approach

Home uses the supplied primary positioning verbatim, then progressively proves it through portfolio context. Supporting sections avoid feature-list language and use concrete advisor actions: analyze, compare, coordinate, review, refine, approve, and prepare. The conclusion is capacity for judgment—not an unsupported productivity statistic.

About does not invent a founder story. It frames the company around a point of view: portfolio strategy and implementation should not live in separate systems. Unverified history, team, location, milestones, careers, and photography remain visibly labeled publication placeholders.

Contact is a demo-request workflow rather than a generic inbox. Success is only shown after a configured service returns success. Without an endpoint, the form reports that the request has not been sent.

## Remotion storyboard — “One Household. One Coordinated Decision.”

The composition runs 23.8 seconds at 30 fps and is silent. It is one continuous, scroll-controlled 3D decision window rather than a set of scene cuts. The outer workspace, navigation rail, selected-household context, and approval path persist while the workspace advances through six advisor actions. Semantic HTML remains layered beside the film for navigation, accessibility, indexing, and responsive copy. The same composition remains independently reusable in Remotion Studio.

1. **Household priorities** — a drift-ranked household library brings the relationships needing attention to the top.
2. **Household analytics** — the selected household persists while allocation, exposure, currency, and model alignment come into view.
3. **Household rebalance** — analysis becomes a projected allocation and proposed account-level buys and sells.
4. **Portfolio-manager review** — material exceptions remain visible and trades advance only after professional review.
5. **Deployment Center** — approved trades are organized into clear tranches while held items remain separate.
6. **Trade export** — the selected tranche, included accounts, and remaining holds are confirmed before the reviewed trades are prepared for export.

## Generic-SaaS critique and revision

The first obvious dark-SaaS direction would have placed glowing metric cards beside a large headline. That would make Laminar look like analytics software and weaken the implementation story. The revised system treats the selected household, analytical panels, proposed trades, review states, and deployment tranches as one continuous advisor workflow controlled by the visitor’s scroll. Blue is used as a system cue, not a glow. Product surfaces remain connected to ruled page structure instead of floating in space.
